Common topics

  • Restore purchases: Open Settings in the App → Restore Purchases (same Apple Account used to buy).
  • Manage subscription: App Store account → Subscriptions, or Settings → Manage Subscription in the App.
  • Study reminders: Optional local notifications; disable in App Settings or iOS Settings.
  • Content offline: The question bank is on-device; no internet is required to practise after install.
